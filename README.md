Moonstone Heist — a top-down stealth game: sneak through a museum at night, past guards with sweeping vision cones, hide in the bushes, grab the gold relics, steal the Moonstone and slip out the green door unseen. Three floors with crossing patrols, a detection meter that gives you a chance to recover, suspicious guards who come to investigate, alarms, chases, and synthesized sound. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make the best stealth game” bounty, $50 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts # then open http://localhost:8080
```

Move with WASD or arrow keys, hold Shift to sneak, P to pause, M to mute.
